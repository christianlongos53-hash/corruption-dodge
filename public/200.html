About Corruption Dodge: Gamifying Civic Transparency

Corruption Dodge is a satirical civic web game created to address one of the most pressing socio-economic challenges in monsoon-prone archipelagos: recurrent catastrophic flooding exacerbated by anomalous infrastructure spending, procurement kickbacks, and ghost projects.

Every monsoon season, thousands of Filipino communities are submerged under murky floodwaters while billions allocated for dikes and pumping stations disappear into substandard works. Corruption Dodge translates this civic struggle into an engaging arcade experience that promotes transparency and public works accountability.

How to Play: Comprehensive Guide & Threat Matrix

Guide your runner along dynamic roadways, dodging corrupt obstacles:

  • ✉️ Bribe Envelopes: Swift under-the-table kickbacks that surge floodwaters by 10%.
  • 💵 Cash Stacks: Illicit disbursements drifting across lanes.
  • 💼 Gold Briefcases: High-level procurement kickbacks dealing heavy damage.
  • 🛢️ Pork Barrels: Substandard bloated projects blocking highway traffic.

Touch infrastructure tokens to pave roads, clear storm drainage, erect river dikes, and install mega pumping stations before 10 hits trigger a catastrophic flood surge.

Character Dossiers & Civic Lore

  • Juan (Citizen): The resilient everyday commuter and taxpayer navigating flooded streets with humor and agility.
  • BingBong (Red President): The charismatic executive dashing forward with bold modernization plans.
  • Sharah (Green Vice President): The disciplined security leader asserting order over roadway chaos.
  • Neli (Pink Mayor): The grassroots reformer mobilizing volunteer flood relief and transparent audits.

Philippine Flood Mitigation Primer

Effective flood mitigation relies on uncompromised civil engineering: reinforced river dikes, automated pumping stations, stormwater retention basins, and mangrove watershed protection. Transparent auditing ensures these public funds save lives.
